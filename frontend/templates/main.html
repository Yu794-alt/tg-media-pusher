Features

Our platform is equipped with a suite of powerful tools designed to streamline and enhance every step of your recruitment process. By leveraging cutting-edge technology, we help you save time, reduce bias, and connect with the best talent more effectively than ever before.
Intelligent CV Review
Streamline your hiring process. Our AI quickly analyzes resumes to identify top talent, match skills, and filter candidates efficiently, saving valuable time and resources.
Seamless User Chatbot
Engage applicants instantly. Our Telegram-based chatbot provides immediate answers to common questions, schedules interviews, and offers personalized support 24/7, improving candidate experience.

Make data-driven decisions. Gain comprehensive analytics on your recruitment funnel, candidate demographics, and chatbot performance to optimize strategies and identify areas for improvement.

Reduce manual workload. Automate routine HR tasks like sending reminders, follow-ups, and updating candidate statuses directly within Telegram, freeing up your team for strategic work.
